## Series and groups

- Series (#177–#179): the series added together form a group (`SeriesSpec::group`, `PanelSpec::push_series_batch` — every new multiple addition of series goes through it); they are set in their own window, `app/curve_settings.rs` (tab `PlotSettingsTab::Series` outside the graph band), and the gradients are `stokesforge_plot::gradient`. A group (#197) has a stored **name** (`PanelSpec::group_names`), stays **contiguous** in `PanelSpec::series` and is handled through the methods of `PanelSpec` (`set_series_group`, `dissolve_group`, `remove_group`, `move_group`…), never by rewriting `group` by hand (removing a single series is `PanelSpec::remove_series`, #257, never `panel.series.remove` in the app); the Y axis of a series (#358) goes through `PanelSpec::set_series_axis` (Right shows the right axis), never `series.axis = …` (the `pyst` `axis` option uses the same method); it is a parent node of the Object Manager and of the series list, both projected by `PanelSpec::slots`. A hover help on a field goes through `app/hover_help.rs` (`hover_help`, `formula_field` for a formula), never through a collapsible note. The `F(x)=` cell of the workbook grid has its own help (#404, `app/column_formula_help.rs`, shown only while it is edited, preference `show_column_formula_help`, separate from `show_formula_help` of the Typst fields): its list is `stokesforge_app_state::formula_help`, an exhaustive `match` on `Callee` (a callee added to the parser does not compile without its help line, its example compiled by a test and its `formula-help-*` description in en and fr).

- Legend text (#499): the text of a series in the legend and in the series lists is **only** `stokesforge_core::series_label(series, tree, &panel.legend.text)`; every list caller passes the legend of the series' own panel (`series_list_label(tree, series, &panel.legend.text)`). A custom template is pure Typst that reads the variables of one table (`stokesforge_plot::VARIABLES`, names identical in every language), defined by a preamble of string literals (`template_source`, `typst_string`); column text never goes into the markup raw. The lists show `render::formula::plain_text` of it, the grey fallback if empty or not compiling. A new « Text from » choice is a `LegendTextFrom` variant with its `key()`, its `legend-text-from-*` key in en and fr, its arm in `series_label` and a line in the `resolve.rs` tests.
- Color bar (#499): the colour of a curve on a bar is **computed at display** from its value and the gradient its group retains (`stokesforge_render::color_bar::value_colors`), **never written to `SeriesSpec`**: removing the bar, hiding it or editing an x' gives the right colors at the next draw. `apply_group_gradient` stays the rank spread; a group put on a bar without a retained gradient gets the first preset in the same edit (`put_group_on_bar`). A group's entries leave the ordinary legend through the one `Prepared::in_legend` predicate (entries and ranks). The bar is read in the graph's group order (`PanelSpec::color_bar_in_order`: a stored order is ignored; the label of a segment is the break after its group). Its page is a folded section at the bottom of the Legend tab (`color_bar_section`, `PlotSettings::color_bar_unfold` for the bar's double-click). A bar edit is a `PanelSpec::color_bar` draft change (`PlotEditKind::ColorBar`); a new keyed choice is a `ColorBar*` variant with `key()`, its `plot-color-bar-*` key in en and fr, and an `Unknown` fallback.
